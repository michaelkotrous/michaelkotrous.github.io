## Hi! My name is Michael <span class="emoji">&#x1F44B;</span>

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the John Munro Godfrey, Sr. Department of Economics at the [University of Georgia](https://www.terry.uga.edu/directory/people/michael-kotrous){:target="_blank"}. My research lies at the intersection of empirical industrial organization and labor economics, with particular interests in franchising and broadband markets.

My latest [working paper](/uploads/research/Kotrous_Michael_MuniBroadband.pdf){:target="_blank"} studies the effects of Chattanooga, Tennessee's municipal broadband project on its local labor market performance.

Before graduate school, I worked as a program manager at the [Mercatus Center at George Mason University](https://www.mercatus.org/people/michael-kotrous){:target="_blank"}, contributing to projects related to regulatory processes, technological innovation, and entrepreneurship. My policy writing and commentary have appeared in outlets including *[USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/opinion/2018/05/02/flight-sharing-flytenow-uber-mike-lee-column/569753002/){:target="_blank"}*, *[POLITICO Magazine](https://www.politico.com/news/magazine/2020/04/02/coronavirus-economy-reopen-deaths-balance-analysis-159248#1){:target="_blank"}*, *[U.S. News &amp; World Report](https://www.usnews.com/opinion/economic-intelligence/articles/2017-10-30/regulating-facebook-like-a-utility-will-further-politicize-media){:target="_blank"}*, *[The Hill](https://thehill.com/social-tags/michael-kotrous){:target="_blank"}*, and *[Tribune News Service](https://web.archive.org/web/20230118050906/https://www.centralmaine.com/2020/05/23/commentary-will-lockdowns-really-kill-more-people-than-covid-19-itself/){:target="_blank"}*.

I also enjoy programming and data-visualization projects, many of which are hosted on [GitHub](https://github.com/michaelkotrous){:target="_blank"}.
